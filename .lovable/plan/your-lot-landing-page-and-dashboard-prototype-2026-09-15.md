# Your Lot landing page and dashboard prototype

## Goal
Build a polished, responsive prototype for a self-managed Australian owners corporation platform, matching the supplied premium fintech reference while using Your Lot’s warm cream and forest-green identity.

## Pages
- **Home (`/`)**: sticky navigation, focused headline and call-to-action, framed dashboard preview, local trust strip, three feature areas, automation callout, pricing preview, committee testimonials, closing call-to-action, and a restrained footer.
- **Dashboard (`/dashboard`)**: desktop sidebar and compact mobile navigation, scheme selector, empty metric tiles, levy chart empty state, compliance checklist, maintenance table, and activity empty state. Sidebar choices will switch the visible dashboard section without fabricated data.
- **Pricing (`/pricing`)**: clear two-plan comparison for Self-Serve and Managed-Lite, annual per-lot pricing treatment, feature checklists, and plan actions.

## Visual system
- Warm cream canvas, charcoal typography, muted forest-green controls, with restrained yellow, blue, and clay labels.
- Geometric Poppins headings with Inter body copy.
- Soft 12–16px corners, fine borders, understated shadows, and a subtle cream grid texture without gradients.
- Consistent shadcn controls and cards, semantic design tokens, restrained motion, and accessible focus states.

## Responsive behaviour
- Preserve the desktop marketing composition while stacking sections cleanly on small screens.
- Replace the dashboard sidebar with a compact mobile menu/navigation.
- Keep controls, tables, and dashboard panels usable without overlap at narrow widths.

## Validation
- Verify all three pages load with their own metadata.
- Test landing, pricing, dashboard navigation, desktop layout, and mobile layout in the running preview.
